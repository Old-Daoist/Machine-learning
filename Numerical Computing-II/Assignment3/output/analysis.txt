For x^3-2x+1, Richardson is already at floating-point-level error at h=1e-1, so a clean O(h^4) regime cannot be resolved numerically in double precision.

Q6. What role does floating-point round-off error play?
  The central-difference numerator subtracts two nearby function values. For very small h, subtractive cancellation removes significant digits before division by 2h.
  Consequently, decreasing h eventually stops reducing total error. The error curves reach minima and then flatten, rise, or become irregular.
  Richardson combines several finite-precision differences, so its higher-order truncation error can be much smaller while round-off/cancellation eventually limits the result.
